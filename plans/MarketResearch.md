# Market research — AI-native sales & marketing SaaS for small/medium Indian builders
*Compiled 18 Oct 2026. Most Indian sources are vendor content (CRM / WhatsApp BSP / agency blogs); figures from them are marked as vendor-estimated. Each claim links its source.*

## Executive summary — what a builder will pay for

1. **"Never sell the same plot twice, and show brokers live inventory."** Double allotments and Excel/PDF-based inventory are a named pain in every Indian inventory-tool pitch (Layouts360, Makanify, BuilderCloud). Incumbent CRMs hide this behind a 5–10 user minimum at ₹700–₹3,000/user/month; a flat per-project price undercuts them.
2. **Stop leaking paid leads.** Sell.do's benchmark: 15–25% of leads on spreadsheet-run teams never get a call; their worked example is ₹96,000/month wasted on ₹4L of lead spend. Speed-to-first-call via WhatsApp is the fix builders understand.
3. **Settle channel-partner (CP) payout disputes with timestamps.** Brokerage is 1–3% (2% metro anchor), 4–6% on aged stock; 4 of 5 common disputes are records problems (first-touch, duplicate registration, base value). CPs drive ~50–60% of sales in MMR per CREDAI-MCHI.
4. **Cost per site visit, not cost per lead.** Tier-2 Meta CPL is ₹150–₹500; lead→site-visit ~10–12%, so a site visit costs ₹1,500–₹5,000 and a booking (~20% visit→booking) ~₹7,500–₹25,000 — versus ₹60,000 CP brokerage on a ₹30L plot.
5. **WhatsApp is the operating system.** BSP plans cost ₹2,500–₹7,000/month; Meta charges ~₹0.86–₹1.09 per marketing message; Click-to-WhatsApp ad replies open a 72-hour free window. An AI qualifier that books site visits in Hindi/English is the clearest "AI" value a non-tech builder will pay for.

## 1. Pain points of small/medium builders

- **Lead leakage and slow follow-up.** Sell.do: leakage of 15–25% "is normal for teams running on spreadsheets"; 500 leads/month at ₹800 → 120 never called → ₹96,000 wasted (~1 lost booking/month at 4% lead→visit, 20% visit→booking). Most leads convert on the 4th–7th touch but teams stop at two; "a lead unassigned for four hours is effectively cold". ([sell.do](https://www.sell.do/blog/real-estate-lead-leakage-brokers-2026)). BuildFormula claims 30–40% of leads lost with no follow-up system ([buildformula](https://buildformula.com/why-indian-builders-are-losing-30-40-of-leads-how-to-stop-it/)). Caller.Digital case: Pune developer where "74% of the funnel never received a single dial" ([caller.digital](https://caller.digital/blog/ai-calling-agent-real-estate-india-2026)).
- **Duplicate leads / portal sharing.** One buyer → 3 records across portal, Meta ad, site visit; Housing/PropTiger/Makaan share a lead pool ([sell.do portals](https://www.sell.do/blog/real-estate-portal-leads-99acres-magicbricks-housing-2026)).
- **Double booking / Excel inventory.** Layouts360: "inventory without booking workflows still leaks double allotments", brokers "sell from PDFs", timed holds/tokens are "essential, especially for launch weekends" ([layouts360](https://layouts360.com/blog/best-real-estate-inventory-management-software-india-2026)). Bhopal consumer forum ordered refund + 18% interest after a builder sold an already-booked flat ([moneylife](https://mas360.moneylife.in/article/bhopal-district-consumer-forum-directs-noted-builder-to-refund-booking-amount-to-home-buyer-nidhi-sharma/2404.html)).
- **Channel-partner commission disputes.** Slabs 1–3%, payout Day 30–120; five common disputes: source conflict, two CPs registering the same buyer, BSP vs all-inclusive base, clawback, slab counting ([sell.do CP](https://www.sell.do/blog/channel-partner-brokerage-payouts-india-2026)). SJR Primecorp put ₹2 cr in escrow (Apr 2026) over delayed broker payouts ([nbmcw](https://www.nbmcw.com/news/real-estate/sjr-primecorp-places-rs-2-cr-in-escrow-to-secure-realtor-commission-payouts.html)). CPs influence 50–60% of MMR transactions ([propnewstime](https://propnewstime.com/latestnewsstories/MzAxNTM=/channel-partners-drive-nearly-60-of-mmr-home-sales-says-credai-mchi-at-first-exclusive-property-expo)).
- **Low CRM penetration.** HomeBuy360 founder (2015): "<10% of developers use CRM" ([realtynmore](https://realtynmore.com/housing-com-acquires-hb360)). No newer figure.
- **Sentiment.** CREDAI–CRE Matrix Dec 2025: 83% of developers expect unsold inventory to sell within two years — pitch "sell faster/cheaper", not "survive".

## 3. What builders pay for marketing

- Meta CPL (India RE): Tier-2/3 affordable ₹150–₹500; premium ₹500–₹2,000; CPM ₹150–₹250 ([leadproio](https://leadproio.com/real-estate-cost-per-lead-india/)); Sell.do: Meta ₹150–₹2,000, Google ₹300–₹2,500. No plot-specific CPL data exists publicly.
- Funnel: lead→site visit ~10–12%; 30 site visits ≈ 300 leads × ₹250 = ₹75,000.
- Click-to-WhatsApp: AiSensy × Adani Realty: CPL −30%, SQLs +30% ([aisensy](https://aisensy.com/case-studies/adani-realty)); reply within 24h opens a 72-hour free window ([Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)).
- WhatsApp Platform (India, 2026): marketing ~₹0.86–₹1.09/msg, utility ₹0.115–₹0.16, service free for first 1,000/month ([montymobile](https://montymobile.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-inr-rates-gst-and-the-2026-currency-migration-deadline), [aisensy](https://aisensy.com/pricing), [interakt](https://www.interakt.shop/pricing/)).

- Agencies: Meta management ₹10,000–₹1.5L/month + spend; reels ₹15–40k package, freelancers ₹800–₹900/reel.

## 6. Willingness to pay and market size

- Anchors: CRM ₹5k–₹30k/month (seat minimums); BSP ₹2.5k–₹7k + Meta fees; agency ₹10k–₹30k retainer + ₹50k–₹2L spend; portals ₹30k–₹60k/month. A 100-plot Tier-2 project selling 5 plots/month via CPs at 2% of ₹30L = ₹3L/month brokerage. **₹15–30k/month for software that saves one booking or one CP dispute is inside the envelope.**
- Size: 1.43 lakh RERA projects / 1.11 crore units (2017–2025), avg 81 units/project (PropEquity); CREDAI ~13,000 developer members. Estimated (unverified) 35k–70k RERA promoters; 15–25k with an active 50+ unit project; realistic Tier-2 SAM ~5–8k builders.

## 7. RERA / DPDP constraints

- RERA s.11(2): every advertisement must carry the authority website + registration number; s.59–61 penalties up to 5% of project cost; s.12 refund + interest for misleading ads. MahaRERA QR code mandatory in every ad incl. social; UP-RERA ₹266 cr penalties; TNRERA (Jul 2026) fines ₹2–5L for missing reg. no./QR, covers social posts and videos; price comparison with "market price" is a major violation. Telangana fined a promoter for an ad a plot buyer placed. **Every generated ad/reel/WhatsApp creative must auto-stamp RERA no., QR, authority URL.**
- "Sold out / limited units": CCPA 2022 guidelines allow scarcity claims only with reasonable supply and disclaimers. **Showing actual live inventory status is the safest scarcity, and a compliance selling point for Stage 1.**
- DPDP Act 2023 / Rules 2025: substantive rules apply ~May 2027; consent must be specific and as easy to withdraw as give; builder = Data Fiduciary, this SaaS = Data Processor (needs a DPA); penalties up to ₹250 cr. TRAI DLT governs promotional SMS/calls. **The name+mobile gate needs a purpose-specific consent line, consent log, withdrawal path, retention rule.**

## Ranked feature list

**Stage 1 — Inventory**
1. Unit-level status (available / on-hold with auto-expiring timer / booked / sold / not for sale / high-demand) on the 2D/3D plan — stops double allotment.
2. Live, read-only broker link per project (no login, WhatsApp-shareable) — replaces the PDF brokers sell from.
3. CP-tagged lead register with first-touch timestamp and validity window, duplicate-phone check — ends 4 of 5 payout disputes.
4. Hold → token → booking workflow with audit trail.
5. Cost sheet + payment schedule per unit, WhatsApp-sendable.
6. Brokerage ledger visible to the CP.

**Stage 2 — Ads & WhatsApp**
1. Instant WhatsApp first response (<1 min) to Meta/portal/website leads.
2. Hindi/English AI qualifier (budget, purpose, timeline, loan) → hot/warm/cold → site-visit slot + reminder.
3. Click-to-WhatsApp campaign launcher using the 72-hour free window.
4. Attribution dashboard: cost per lead → site visit → booking by source and CP.
5. Drip nurture for cold leads.
6. Owner's daily WhatsApp digest.

**Stage 3 — Content**
1. Auto-generated plot/unit reels from the 3D model with price band + RERA stamp.
2. AI renders per unit type.
3. Weekly Instagram/YouTube Shorts calendar with a compliance checker.
4. CP co-branded creatives.
